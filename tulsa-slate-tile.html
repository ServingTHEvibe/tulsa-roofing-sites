What We Do Best

The Art of Slate & Tile Roofing

Natural Slate Roofing

The most durable roofing material on earth. We source premium Vermont and Spanish slate and install it with museum-quality precision. Properly installed slate lasts 100–150 years.

Clay & Concrete Tile

From Mediterranean barrel tile to modern flat profiles, we install every style with proper flashing and underlayment. Your tile roof, done right the first time.

Specialty Repair & Restoration

Most roofers pass on slate repairs. We don't. Whether it's a handful of broken tiles or a full restoration of a historic home, we have the expertise and the matching materials.

Free Expert Inspection

Not sure if your slate roof needs repairs or full replacement? We'll give you an honest assessment — no pressure, no upsell. Just 20 years of honest expertise.

Historic Home Specialist

Midtown Tulsa's historic homes deserve historically accurate roofing. We work with preservation guidelines and source period-appropriate materials when needed.

Insurance Claim Support

Hail and storm damage on a slate or tile roof requires a specialist's eye. We document damage correctly, communicate with adjusters, and ensure you get what your policy entitles you to.
