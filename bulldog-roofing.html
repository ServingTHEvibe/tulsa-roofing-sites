What We Do Best

Full-Service Roofing.
Zero Shortcuts.

Roof Replacement

Complete tear-off and re-roof with premium materials. We've replaced hundreds of roofs in Bixby — and every single one comes with a written workmanship warranty.

Hail & Storm Damage

South Tulsa gets hammered by hail. We respond fast, document everything, and handle the insurance claim process from inspection through payment. Most homeowners pay only their deductible.

Roof Repair

Leaks, missing shingles, failed flashing — we fix it right the first time. Our repairs aren't band-aids; they're long-term solutions backed by our workmanship guarantee.

Gutters & Downspouts

Seamless aluminum gutters installed properly make a huge difference in roof longevity. We size them correctly, slope them right, and make sure every drop goes where it should.

Attic Ventilation

Poor attic ventilation kills shingles early and drives up energy bills. We assess your current setup and install ridge vents, soffit vents, and attic fans to protect your investment.

Free Inspection

Every estimate starts with a free, no-pressure inspection. We get on the roof, take photos, and walk you through exactly what we find. No surprises, no pressure.
