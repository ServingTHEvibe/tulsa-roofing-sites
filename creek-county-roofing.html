What We Do

Honest Roofing.
Creek County Dependable.

Roof Replacement

Full tear-off and replacement using premium architectural shingles. Every job comes with a comprehensive workmanship warranty and a property that's left cleaner than we found it.

Storm & Hail Damage

We've helped hundreds of Creek County families navigate hail damage claims. We know the insurance process and fight to make sure you get the full settlement your policy covers.

Roof Repair

Leak repairs, flashing replacement, ridge work, vent boots — we fix what's broken and tell you when something can wait. Honest assessments, no manufactured urgency.

Gutters & Drainage

Seamless aluminum gutters installed correctly protect your fascia, foundation, and landscaping. We size them for your roof, hang them at the right pitch, and they drain properly.

Attic Ventilation

Oklahoma summers are brutal on shingles. Proper ventilation — ridge vents, soffit vents, or attic fans — can add years to your roof's lifespan and cut your cooling bills.

Free Inspection

A real inspection, on the roof, with photos and an honest report. No pressure, no obligation. Just good information from a family business that plans to be here for another 20 years.
